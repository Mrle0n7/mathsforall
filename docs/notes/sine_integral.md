# Proof of Wallis' Integral

The Wallis integral occupies a central place in classical analysis. First studied by John Wallis in the 17th century, it provides an exact evaluation of a family of definite integrals involving powers of the sine and cosine functions.

This document presents a detailed proof of the Wallis integral:

\[
\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x \; dx
\]

for non-negative integer $n$.
