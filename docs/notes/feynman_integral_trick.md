# Feynman Integral Trick 

The Feynman Integral Trick is a clever method for evaluating definite integrals that are hard to solve by standard techniques. It’s named after physicist Richard Feynman, who popularized the method in solving tricky integrals during his studies. 

Instead of directly computing a difficult integral, we introduce a parameter, differentiate with respect to it inside the integral, and then solve the resulting simpler problem. Afterward, we integrate with respect to the parameter to recover the original answer.

This method can turn a stubborn integral into a much easier one, sometimes even leading to integrals that are impossible to do by hand otherwise.
